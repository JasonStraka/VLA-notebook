# Awesome VLA Papers

> A public, GitHub-ready bibliography of Vision-Language-Action models and closely related robot-learning papers.  
> **Updated: 2026-10-15**

This document is intentionally limited to public papers, technical reports, paper-backed benchmarks, and directly relevant foundation-model papers. The classification is methodological rather than a ranking.

## Scope and notation

- Each paper has one **primary placement** to avoid duplicate counting. Closely related directions are connected through cross-indexes.
- “Project” or “Code” links are included only when a public entry was available in the curated source or was verified during this revision.
- Preprints and technical reports are listed as such by their public source; inclusion is not an endorsement of empirical claims.
- The collection spans VLA architecture, action generation, temporal execution, spatial representation, world models, memory, cross-embodiment transfer, RL/post-training, data/simulation, and evaluation.

## 06. Adaptive action chunking & execution horizons

动作块执行长度、切换时机、连续性和可靠性驱动的自适应 horizon。

<a id="p059"></a>

### Knowing When to Stop

**Knowing When to Stop: Adaptive Action Chunking via Internal Cross-Attention Dynamics in VLAs**

[论文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00908)

**Summary:** 利用 action-to-observation cross-attention 熵的变化作为训练免费信号，自适应截断 action chunk。

<a id="p060"></a>

### BCP

**Continue or Replan? Bernoulli-Continuation Policy Learning for Adaptive Horizon Execution**

[论文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03483) · [项目](https://fleetfootwork.github.io/BCP/)

**Summary:** 将执行 horizon 选择分解为一系列 continue-or-replan 决策，并通过轨迹级强化学习兼顾任务成功和重规划效率。

<a id="p061"></a>

### RACE

**When to Switch: Reliable Action-Chunk Extension for Vision-Language-Action Models**

[论文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05719) · [代码](https://github.com/Seonghoon-Yu/RACE-VLA)

**Summary:** 分析长 action chunk 的误差集中于子技能转换，并显式预测 transition timing 来支持更长且更可靠的 chunk。

<a id="p062"></a>

### CA³C

**Commit While Futures Agree: Consequence-Aware Adaptive Action Chunking for Robot Manipulation**

[论文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07949)

**Summary:** 用动作条件世界模型想象多个候选 chunk 的未来后果，并以未来共识和变化点推断决定可安全提交的执行长度。

<a id="p063"></a>

### GeoAAC

**GeoAAC: Geometry-Based Adaptive Action Chunking from Denoising Trajectories in VLA Policies**

[论文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20776)

**Summary:** 从 Flow Matching 去噪轨迹几何中提取 prefix-wise 可靠性信号，在单次生成内自适应选择 action horizon。

<a id="p064"></a>

### SEAM

**SEAM: Smooth Execution of Action-Chunked Motion for Vision-Language-Action Policies**
